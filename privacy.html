Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Discord application Eko ("the Bot") collects, how it's used, and what rights you have over it. By adding the Bot to a Discord server, you agree to the practices described here.

1. What data we collect

The Bot collects and stores only the data necessary for it to function:

DataWhat it's used for
Discord user IDIdentify your economy, level, and inventory data within each server
Server (guild) IDKeep each server's data separate from one another
Virtual currency balanceEconomy system (/balance, /daily, /work, /pay...)
Experience (XP) and levelLeveling system (/rank, /ranktop)
Message countBattle pass progress (/battlepass)
Items purchased in the shopManage your inventory (/inventory) and apply roles/badges
Timestamp of your last actions (/daily, /work, /game)Enforce cooldowns between uses
Configured welcome channel IDKnow where to send the message when a new member joins

The Bot does NOT collect or store the content of your messages, email addresses, IP addresses, or any personal data beyond your Discord identifier.

2. Avatar and username

When you join a server, the Bot temporarily displays your name and profile picture (obtained directly from Discord) in the welcome message. This information is not stored in our database; it's fetched at the moment and not retained.

3. How we store data

Data is stored in a private database, hosted on the server running the Bot, and is not shared with or sold to any third party. Periodic backups are made solely for disaster-recovery purposes.

4. Who we share data with

We do not share your data with third parties, except:

  • Discord Inc., which processes communication between the Bot and its servers under its own Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe, only if you purchase a premium subscription — Discord handles that payment directly and the Bot never has access to your payment data (card details, billing, etc.).

5. How long we keep data

We retain your data for as long as the Bot remains in the server you participate in. If a server's administrator removes the Bot, or if you explicitly request it (see Section 7), the data associated with that server is deleted.

6. Minors

The Bot is subject to Discord's Terms of Service, which require a minimum age of 13 to use the platform. We do not intentionally collect additional data from minors beyond what's already described in this policy.

7. Your rights

You can request at any time:

  • Access to the data we hold about you (your balance, level, inventory, etc. — you can check these yourself with the /balance, /rank, and /inventory commands).
  • Deletion of your data, by contacting the developer through the Bot's official support server or by email.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Significant changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, or to exercise your rights, contact the developer through the Bot's official support server or by email.
